v1.0.36 2026-06-30

June 30 - 180db v1.0.35

Features

  • Cue — CDJ-style cueing. Drop a cue point, preview from it, and launch playback from exactly that spot.

  • C sets and triggers the cue. Hold C to preview from the cue point; release to snap back to it.

  • With C held, Space launches playback from the cue point.

  • X clears the cue.

  • , and . (< > buttons) nudge the cue point backward and forward in fine steps. If no cue is set yet, the first nudge drops one at the playhead.

  • Shift-, and Shift-. make larger jumps.

  • = flash-zooms the waveform to the cue, or to the playhead when there's no cue. Pressing again will get back to the normal view

  • Transient mode — Turn on Audio > Transient Detection and 180db analyzes the track's transients. With it on, Shift-, and Shift-. snap the cue to the previous and next transient instead of nudging, so you can place cues precisely on the hit.

  • Album covers in the playlist — Turn on View > Show Cover Image to show album art in your playlist rows, with a placeholder for tracks that have no artwork.

Quality of Life

  • Faster scrolling and loading on large playlists. Cover thumbnails and waveforms are now cached and reused, and refresh automatically when a file changes on disk.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed audio clicks and pops around cue points and track changes.

v1.0.11 2026-05-22

May 22 - v1.0.11

Features

  • Shader animation toggle — Added View > Shader Animation to turn the background shader and DJ ripple animations on or off.

  • Click-to-position volume — Single-click anywhere on the volume slider to jump to that volume level. Double-click to toggle mute still works.

  • Option-click to close other windows — Option-click the close button on a player window to close all other player windows while keeping that one open.

  • Dock icon opens a new window — Clicking the dock icon when no windows are open now opens a fresh empty player window.

Quality of Life

  • Larger album cover — Maximum album cover size increased from 320px to 600px.

  • Manage License moved to app menu — "Manage License" / "Buy Full Version" moved from the Help menu to the 180db app menu, next to "Check for Updates".

  • Other minor improvements

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed window controls when unfocused — The floating close and minimize buttons now work correctly when clicking on a background (non-key) window.

  • Fixed transport not updating when paused — Switching tracks with next/previous while paused no longer shows stale time and duration from the previous track.

  • Fixed volume drag outside window — Dragging the volume slider no longer stops tracking when the cursor leaves the window bounds. The resize cursor also stays visible during the drag.

  • Fixed drag and drop in compact mode — Drag-and-dropping audio files onto the player now works even when the album cover, playlist, and status bar are all hidden.

  • Fixed cover size not restoring after toggle — Toggling the album cover off and on no longer shrinks the window to a smaller size than before.

v1.0.37 2026-07-03

180db Changelog — July 3, 2026

Stability

  • Fixed several crashes: moving tracks with nothing selected, pressing play on a restored window whose playlist had shrunk, and loading playlists with stale database entries.
  • Closing the license window with the red button no longer leaves the app stuck with no windows.
  • Pressing Stop exactly as a track ends no longer jumps to the next track on its own.

Playlists

  • Skipped and cycling track markers now survive reordering, deleting tracks, and app restarts — previously any playlist change silently wiped them.
  • Moving a non-contiguous selection now keeps the right tracks highlighted.
  • Re-importing files you already have no longer erases their titles, artists, and metadata.

Startup & Windows

  • The app now launches instantly when offline or on a slow network — no more waiting on the license server.
  • Window positions are saved reliably, even when you move a window and quit immediately.
  • Opening windows is faster (fewer database reads per window).

System Integration

  • Control Center now shows a live progress scrubber and updates when an album advances between tracks.
  • Opening an audio file from Finder into an empty window now imports and starts playing reliably.
  • Captured audio (Cmd+B) pasted as a file no longer disappears after 60 seconds.

Multi-Window

  • Right-click menus on a background window now act on that window — previously Pause or Remove Track could hit the wrong player.

Keyboard

  • Shortcuts like C (cue), M (mute), and S (shuffle) now work on non-QWERTY keyboard layouts.
  • Setting a cue at the very start of a track (0:00) now works like any other cue.
  • Raising the volume slider while muted now unmutes.

Safety

  • Wipe Database now asks for confirmation before deleting anything — Return and Escape both cancel; the wipe requires an explicit click.

Performance

  • Large album covers no longer re-upload to the GPU on every interaction (big win with the DJ shader active).
  • Playlists with tracks that have no artwork no longer hammer the disk while scrolling.
  • Reduced idle background work: cached text measurements, smarter prefetch polling, batched playlist refreshes.

Under the Hood

  • Removed over 4,000 lines of dead code across the audio engine and app.
  • Build pipeline hardened: a failed Rust build can no longer silently ship a stale audio engine, and release builds now hard-fail if the update signature is missing.
  • New engine safety guards around cue points, decode threads, and device changes.

Requires macOS 14.0 or later
